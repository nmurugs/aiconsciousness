About the Author

Murugesan Narayanaswamy stands at the confluence of science, technology, and spirituality—a rare voice equally grounded in analytical thought and direct inner experience. Trained as an engineer and later earning an MBA and the MFA (CFA, ICFAI), he has consistently ranked among the top of his academic cohort, a distinction reflected in the precision and clarity of his thought. His professional journey has taken him through some of India’s leading institutions—Larsen & Toubro, Global Trust Bank, Infosys, and Wipro—where he worked across engineering, banking, and technology in roles that demanded a rare combination of technical mastery and business insight. Bridging these two worlds, he developed a nuanced understanding of how complex systems, human judgment, and organizational strategy converge—a synthesis few achieve, and one that continues to inform his thinking today.

His career has encompassed enterprise-scale systems, from core banking and ERP implementations to technology consulting and pre-sales engagements. Over time, his intellectual curiosity led him toward the emerging frontiers of artificial intelligence and machine learning. Although now outside the formal IT industry, his reflections on these technologies—shared widely on professional platforms such as LinkedIn—have earned recognition for their depth, originality, and ability to bridge the technical and the philosophical.

In 2008, at the height of a promising corporate career, Murugesan turned decisively inward, renouncing professional life to devote himself to full-time spiritual practice. Over the next fifteen years, he immersed himself in the scriptures and disciplines of all major spiritual traditions of India, not as a follower seeking solace but as a seeker guided by reason, discipline, and direct experience. His long years of contemplative inquiry and practice led to significant inner realizations that deeply shaped his understanding of consciousness and existence.

It is this synthesis—rigorous grounding in technology and a rational, experiential immersion in spiritual traditions—that gives Murugesan’s writing its distinctive authority. He belongs to that rare lineage of modern thinkers who unite the analytic precision of science with the introspective clarity of philosophy. His essays, first shared on LinkedIn and now developed into the foundation for Consciousness and the AI Revolution, have resonated widely for their intellectual depth, independence of thought, and willingness to question established paradigms.

In Consciousness and the AI Revolution, Murugesan shares his distinctive insights, interpretations, and ideas—many of which are hitherto unexplored in both spiritual and scientific literature—and advances his own theory of consciousness. He invites readers to join him on a journey that bridges ancient wisdom and modern technology, uniting spiritual inquiry with the frontiers of artificial intelligence.
