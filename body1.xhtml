A Dedication


To Lord Śiva, the Source and End of All.
Once I was but ordinary consciousness-stuff,
a mirror clouded, catching every fleeting shadow of the world—
the noble and the base, the passing and the trivial.
By His hand, each blemish was chiseled away.


In but fifteen years, He sculpted me
into a polished mirror,
so that I might reflect Him—
not as another,
but as Himself,
indistinguishable from His own radiance.


What offering could I return,
save to let His reflection remain forever unbroken within me?
